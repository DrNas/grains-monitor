Rates & policy

New York Fed reference rates (published 8am ET for the previous day) and the U.S. Treasury daily par yield curve (after the close). Futures-implied policy paths need futures prices and come later.

Treasury liquidity

Treasury's cash at the Fed (TGA) and bill supply. A rising TGA and heavy net bill issuance pull cash out of money markets and bank reserves; a falling TGA puts it back.
