Supply & demand

USDA's monthly WASDE. Change = this month's report against last month's: read the revisions, not the levels.

Crop progress & condition

USDA's weekly Crop Progress (Mondays, 4pm ET): national condition ratings and planting-to-harvest progress, this season against last season and the average of the previous five. The yield model reads the condition index at the point in the season where it has best predicted the final yield.

Exports

USDA's weekly export sales (Thursdays, 8:30am ET) and export inspections (Mondays, 11am ET), each against USDA's WASDE export forecast and the usual seasonal pace of the previous five marketing years.
