Look-back Crop year

Desk brief

A written read of this week's CFTC report: managed money (funds) against producers and merchants (commercials), in every Bloomberg Commodity Index market the CFTC reports. Positioning, not a price forecast.

Trade ideas

Systematic rules, research first and positioning second: trend (12-month return confirmed by 6-month and the 200-day), carry (front vs the same month next year), inverse-volatility size, halved in an equity risk-off regime; crowded positioning cuts trend trades in half, and contrarian setups become ideas only once price confirms. Stops 2×ATR, targets 2R. A systematic read, not advice.

Positioning monitor

Every market ranked by setup score (−100 to +100): crowding by COT index, commercial positioning, and whether fund flow has started to turn. Click a market for its history.

Triggers & flows
